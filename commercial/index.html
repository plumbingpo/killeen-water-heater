Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Killeen Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.
